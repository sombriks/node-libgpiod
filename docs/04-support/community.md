# Support channels

The best place to ask for help is the [discussion forum at github][discussion].

[discussion]: https://github.com/sombriks/node-libgpiod/discussions

## Related channels

Sometimes you can get help on discord channels related to the *SBC ecosystem*,
like [raspberry][pi] or [radxa][radxa] discord servers.

[pi]: https://discord.com/invite/hNkdtbH
[radxa]: https://discord.com/invite/mn73YNWdHY

## Label your project at github

Add `node-libgpiod` as a label in your project, that helps it to be more
discoverable.
